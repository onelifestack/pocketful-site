How it works

Three small steps.

  1. Pick what to learn

    Start with a ready-made deck or make your own. Add cards in a few taps.

  2. Swipe through today's cards

    See the question, flip for the answer, then swipe how it felt.

  3. Come back tomorrow

    Pocketful has already lined up what you are about to forget. A few minutes is plenty.

Already use Anki?

Bring your decks with you.

Pocketful opens standard Anki deck files (.apkg), old and new formats, and keeps the text, pictures and sounds.

  1. Download a deck, for example from AnkiWeb.
  2. Open the file from your Downloads or tap it in your browser, and choose Pocketful.
  3. Check the preview, then tap Import. Your cards are ready to swipe.

Pocketful is independent and not affiliated with Anki. Check you have the right to use any deck you import.

Prefer spreadsheets?

Paste a list or import a CSV file, match the columns and go. Export any deck back to CSV whenever you like.

Switching phones?

Save one backup file with everything in it and restore it on the new phone.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Pocketful free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscriptions and nothing to buy.

Where are my cards stored?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. There is no account and no cloud. We run no servers for Pocketful, so we cannot see your cards.

What if I lose or change my phone?

Use You > Back up to a file to save everything, photos and voice clips included, to a place you choose. On the new phone, tap "Coming from another phone?" and pick that file.

Can I use my Anki decks?

Yes. Download a deck (an .apkg file) from AnkiWeb or anywhere else, then open it with Pocketful or choose Import > An Anki deck file. Text, pictures and sounds come across; cloze cards are split into questions.

Does it sync between devices?

Not in this version. Nothing leaves your phone unless you save a backup file.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8.0 (Oreo) and newer.

Who is it for?

Anyone aged 13 and over who wants to learn words, facts or anything else, a few minutes at a time.

How do I get help or share an idea?

Open You > Help in the app, or write to hello@onelifestack.com.

How do I delete everything?

You > Erase everything in the app, or just uninstall it. There is nothing to delete anywhere else.

Still stuck? Contact support.
