Pocketful: remember what you learn, 2 minutes a day.

Swipe a card right if you knew it, left if you didn't, up if it's too easy. Pocketful brings each card back just before you'd forget it. Make decks with text, photos and your own voice, or start from ready-made ones.

No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone.
