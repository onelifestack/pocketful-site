Press kit

Pocketful is a free, private flashcards app for Android. For anything else, write to hello@onelifestack.com.

In one line

Swipe through a few flashcards a day; Pocketful brings back what you are about to forget, and keeps everything on your phone.

Facts

  • Name: Pocketful
  • Maker: OneLifeStack
  • Platform: Android 8.0 and newer
  • Price: free, no ads, no purchases
  • Audience: ages 13 and over
  • Privacy: no account, no analytics, no internet permission
